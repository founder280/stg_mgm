Mass Gathering Health Management System

Health services for a mass gathering: an administration console for the state, a live GIS and surveillance dashboard, and an offline-first field app for temporary medical camps.

▲ This is a demonstration, not a live system. GitHub Pages serves static files, so there is no server and no database here. Both apps carry a snapshot of the seeded gathering and compute everything — syndrome classification, triage, outbreak detection, the spatial scan, stock projection — in your browser, using the very same shared code the real API runs. Nothing you change is saved; reload and it is back.

The Sammakka–Saralamma Jatara at Medaram, Mulugu district, with a zoned gathering area, staff for every role, and a waterborne diarrhoea outbreak planted in one sector over the final three days — so the detectors have something real to find rather than something announced.

What to try, in about three minutes

  1. Open the console as state.admin. In Surveillance signals, look for Acute Diarrhoeal Disease at Jatara North Medical Camp — roughly 30 cases against under 2 expected, flagged by several independent detectors.
  2. Click that syndrome's bar. Every panel narrows at once. On the map, the same sector carries the largest circles and a cluster ring: a temporal detector and a spatial scan agreeing, independently.
  3. Sign out and back in as district.mulugu, then district.bhupalpally. Each sees only their own district. As jatn1.sup, the roles console is not even in the navigation.
  4. Open the field app as jatn1.vol1 and register a walk-in with Fever and Bleeding from gums. Before you forward it, the app names the syndrome and cites IDSP.
  5. Then turn your network off and keep going. Register another patient. Reload the page. It comes back, still signed in, with your records intact.

Running the real thing

The demonstration cannot show what needs a server: PostgreSQL, the scheduled surveillance pass, real sync between devices, or authentication. For that, run the stack — it is three commands with Docker, or open the repository in GitHub Codespaces, where the API, the database and both apps come up in your browser with nothing to install.
